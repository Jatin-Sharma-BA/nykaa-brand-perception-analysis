## 📌 Project Overview

Nykaa is one of India's leading online beauty and fashion retailers. This project analyzes Nykaa's **brand perception, customer sentiment, market trends, and competitive positioning** using data collected from social media platforms and customer review sites.

The goal was to assess Nykaa's online reputation, identify key pain points and strengths, and deliver **5 strategic business recommendations** to improve its market position.

---

## 🧩 Business Problem

Nykaa operates in a fast-growing, highly competitive beauty and fashion market. Despite being a market leader, the company needed deeper insights into:
- How customers perceive the brand online
- What drives positive and negative reviews
- Where competitors are gaining ground
- What emerging trends Nykaa should capitalize on

## 🔧 Methodology

### Step 1 — Data Collection
- Gathered data from social media platforms: Twitter, Instagram, Facebook
- Collected customer reviews from: Google Reviews, Trustpilot, Nykaa product pages
- Analyzed 3,000+ customer reviews and social media mentions
- Identified relevant hashtags and keyword themes

### Step 2 — Brand Perception Analysis
- Reviewed social media posts and customer feedback
- Identified common descriptors — both positive and negative
- Mapped overall brand sentiment

### Step 3 — Customer Review Classification
- Categorized all reviews as **Positive**, **Negative**, or **Neutral**
- Identified major drivers of each sentiment category
- Quantified review distribution

### Step 4 — Market Trends Identification
- Monitored social media for emerging beauty & fashion trends
- Tracked influencer content and trending topics
- Identified key shifts in consumer preferences

### Step 5 — Competitive Landscape Assessment
- Benchmarked Nykaa against 3 key competitors across 4 dimensions
- Identified Nykaa's competitive advantages and gaps

### Step 6 — Strategic Recommendations
- Developed 5 data-backed actionable strategies

---

## 💡 Key Findings

### 📊 Customer Review Distribution

| Sentiment | Percentage |
|---|---|
| ✅ Positive Reviews | 65% |
| 😐 Neutral Reviews | 20% |
| ❌ Negative Reviews | 15% |

### 👍 Top Drivers of Positive Reviews
- Authentic branded products
- Wide product variety
- Attractive discounts and offers
- Good packaging quality

### 👎 Top Drivers of Negative Reviews
- Delivery delays in certain locations
- Slow customer service response time
- Complex return and refund process
- Limited support during peak periods

---

## 📈 Market Trends Identified

- Growing demand for skincare products
- Rising popularity of Korean beauty (K-beauty)
- Shift toward premium cosmetics
- Influencer-driven purchasing decisions
- Increasing awareness of sustainable beauty

## ⚔️ Competitive Analysis

| Factor | Nykaa | Competitors |
|---|---|---|
| Beauty Specialization | ✅ Strong | Moderate |
| Product Authenticity | ✅ High | Medium |
| Discounts & Offers | Moderate | High |
| Brand Trust | ✅ Strong | Moderate |

**Key insight:** Nykaa's specialization in beauty products gives it a significant competitive advantage over generalist platforms like Amazon Fashion and Flipkart.

---

## ✅ Strategic Recommendations

| # | Recommendation | Business Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Improve Delivery Speed & Logistics** | Directly addresses top negative review driver — delivery delays |
| 2 | **Strengthen Customer Support Services** | Reduce response time, especially during peak sale periods |
| 3 | **Expand Premium Product Offerings** | Capitalize on shift toward premium cosmetics trend |
| 4 | **Collaborate with Influencers for Targeted Marketing** | Leverage influencer-driven purchasing behavior |
| 5 | **Use Personalized Recommendations** | Enhance customer experience and increase repeat purchase rate |

**Key Recommendation:** Enhancing operational efficiency (logistics + support) can significantly strengthen Nykaa's leadership position while protecting its strong brand trust.
